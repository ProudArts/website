How to use: Compare vehicles of the same make, model, or year. This tool calculates the "unit price" for every mile left in the vehicle's expected life.

Method: We subtract current mileage from the maximum lifespan to find Miles Left. We then divide the price by those miles to find the base Price Per Mile. Optional fuel and maintenance costs can be added to create a more complete ownership cost per mile. The vehicle with the best value is highlighted in green.

Maintenance Curve: When enabled, maintenance is estimated at $0.04/mile from 0–50,000 miles, $0.08/mile from 50,000–100,000, $0.12/mile from 100,000–150,000, and $0.16/mile above 150,000 miles. The calculator applies the appropriate rates across the vehicle's remaining lifespan and uses the resulting average maintenance cost per mile.

Fuel Cost: When enabled, enter each vehicle's estimated MPG. Fuel cost per mile is calculated using the gas price divided by MPG and added to the vehicle's total cost per mile.

Overpaid: This represents the total extra cash penalty you absorb across that vehicle's remaining miles compared to the best value vehicle in the list.
