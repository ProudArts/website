#10 Business: 500+ Employees Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Walmart, Facebook 21,000 Businesses $500K to $100M+ per year 0.001% You built something that can Operate at Massive Scale, Timing, Empire Builder Mindset

#8 Entertainment Athletes, Actors, Musicians, YouTubers, Streamers, Game Developers 2 Million Plus $0 to $100M+ per year 1% Hit-driven, Winner-Take-All Economy. Star or Starving Artist.
