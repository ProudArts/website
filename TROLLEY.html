About The Trolley Problem and This Trolley Test

The Trolley Problem is a foundational thought experiment in ethics, challenging decision makers to evaluate net consequences versus direct action, This interactive Trolley Game acts as an empirical Trolley Test, requiring switch operators to divert runaway train cars across eleven locked moral dilemmas, weighing aggregate casualties, personal duties, and systemic consequences,
